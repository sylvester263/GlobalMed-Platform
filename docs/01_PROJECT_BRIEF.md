# 01 — Project Brief

## Client
GlobalMed Transcriptions and Billing Solutions — globalmedtranscriptions.com
Services: medical billing, medical coding, medical transcription, AI-powered clinical documentation, medical billing software/service, medical education.

## Why this project
GlobalMed is growing from a service company into a service company **plus** an education institute, supported by a planned strategic partnership with AAPC (aapc.com). The current site cannot sell courses, host lessons, run exams, issue certificates, or capture and qualify leads at scale.

## Goals (measurable)
| Goal | Target within 6 months of launch |
|---|---|
| Qualified service leads from US practices | 30+ per month |
| Course enrollments | 100+ paid enrollments |
| Chatbot-handled first-contact questions | 60%+ resolved without staff |
| Lighthouse (mobile) | ≥ 90 performance, accessibility, best practices, SEO |
| Certificate verification | 100% of issued certificates verifiable online |

## Audiences
1. **US practice owners / office managers** — want reliable billing, fewer denials, faster payments, HIPAA-aware partner.
2. **Career-changers and students** (Pakistan, US, Gulf) — want a job-ready billing/coding skill, a recognised certificate and exam preparation (e.g. for CPC).
3. **Working coders** — want CEU-style refreshers and specialty courses.
4. **Hiring partners** — want trained coders.

## In scope
- Multi-page marketing website (structure inspired by aapc.com, own brand)
- School of Billing and Coding: catalog, course pages, checkout, video lessons with rewatch, progress tracking, quizzes, mock exams, certificates with public verification
- Dashboards: student, instructor, admin (with sales/CRM view)
- AI chatbot on website + WhatsApp (client supplies LLM API key)
- Blog/resources, SEO/AEO/GEO setup, analytics, marketing landing pages
- Email notifications and transactional flows
- Launch marketing campaign assets (landing pages, tracking) — campaign execution scoped separately if needed

## Out of scope (unless change request)
- Handling PHI / patient documents through the website (transcription file intake portal)
- Native mobile apps
- Live classroom video (Zoom/Meet links supported; no custom live streaming)
- Official AAPC exam delivery (AAPC runs its own exams; we prepare students)

## Commercials
- Total: **USD 80,000**
- 50% advance (USD 40,000) on signing; 50% (USD 40,000) after full development and client acceptance
- Third-party running costs (hosting, Supabase, Bunny, Stripe fees, Resend, LLM usage, WhatsApp conversations) billed to the client's own accounts

## Key constraints
- AAPC branding may only be used with AAPC's written permission and within their partner guidelines.
- Chatbot uses the client's own LLM API key.
- Must be manageable by non-technical GlobalMed staff after handover.
