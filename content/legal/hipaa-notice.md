GlobalMed provides billing, coding, transcription and clinical documentation services to US healthcare practices. In that role we are a **business associate** under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

## How we protect patient information for clients

- We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every client before accessing protected health information (PHI).
- We work on PHI only inside our clients' systems and GlobalMed's HIPAA-compliant environment.
- Staff complete HIPAA training when they join and every year after.
- Access to PHI is limited to staff who need it for the work, and access is logged.
- We report security incidents to affected clients as our BAAs and the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule require.

## This website does not handle patient information

This website, the GlobalMed School of Billing and Coding and our chatbot are not part of our PHI-handling environment. They are designed not to collect patient information:

- No form asks for patient details.
- Every form and the chatbot display the notice "Do not include patient information."
- Files for billing or transcription are never exchanged through this website.

If you accidentally submit patient information through this website, email us straight away and we will delete it.
