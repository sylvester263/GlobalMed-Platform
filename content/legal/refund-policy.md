## Bundles and pathways

Bundles are refundable on the same terms, based on your progress across all courses in the bundle.

## How to request a refund

Email us from the address on your account with your order number. We reply within two business days.

## How refunds are paid

- **Card payments:** refunded to the original card through Stripe. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
- **Bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa:** refunded to an account in your name that you confirm with us.

When a refund is issued, access to the course ends and any certificate for that course is revoked.
