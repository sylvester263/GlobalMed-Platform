Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use as few as we can.

## Strictly necessary

Needed for the site to work, such as keeping you signed in and protecting forms against spam (Cloudflare Turnstile). These are always on.

## Analytics (with your consent)

Google Analytics helps us understand which pages are useful. It runs only if you accept analytics cookies.

## Marketing (with your consent)

The Meta Pixel and Google advertising tags measure whether our ads lead to enquiries or enrollments. They run only if you accept marketing cookies.

## Your choices

You can accept or reject analytics and marketing cookies in the cookie banner and change your choice at any time through the "Cookie settings" link. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, although signing in needs strictly necessary cookies.

## Session storage

We keep campaign (UTM) details in your browser's session storage for the current visit, so we can see which campaign brought an enquiry. It's cleared when you close the tab.
