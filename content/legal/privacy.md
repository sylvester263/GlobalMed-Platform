This policy explains what personal information GlobalMed Transcriptions and Billing Solutions ("GlobalMed", "we") collects through this website and GlobalMed Education, and how we use it.

## This website does not collect patient information

Our website, learning platform and chatbot are not designed to receive protected health information (PHI). Please do not enter any patient information into our forms or chat. Clients exchange patient files only through GlobalMed's HIPAA-compliant systems, under a Business Associate Agreement. See our [HIPAA notice](/legal/hipaa-notice).

## Information we collect

- **Enquiries:** your name, work email, phone number, practice or organisation name, specialty and the details you enter in our audit or contact forms.
- **Student accounts:** your name, the name to appear on certificates, email, country, phone number and profile photo if you add one.
- **Learning activity:** lessons watched, playback position, quiz and exam attempts, and certificates issued.
- **Payments:** order details and payment status. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers. For bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa, we store the proof of payment you upload.
- **Chat conversations:** messages you send to our website chatbot or WhatsApp number.
- **Technical data:** device, browser and pages visited, and, with your consent, analytics and advertising cookies.

## How we use it

To respond to enquiries, provide courses and certificates, process payments, provide support, improve our services, send the newsletter you confirmed, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

## Who we share it with

Service providers that run parts of our platform on our behalf: hosting (Vercel), database and authentication (Supabase), video (Bunny.net), payments (Stripe), email (Resend), our chatbot's AI provider, WhatsApp (Meta) and analytics (Google). Each receives only what it needs.

## Certificate verification

Anyone with a certificate ID can see the name on the certificate, the course, the issue date and whether it is valid. That is the purpose of verification.
